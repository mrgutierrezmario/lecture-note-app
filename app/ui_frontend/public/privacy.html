Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

AI Lecture Notes is self-hosted software: the copy you are using runs on a server operated by the person or organisation who gave you your account (“the operator”). This page explains what the app stores, where it goes, and what you control.

What the app stores

  • Account details — your username, email address and a hashed password.
  • Recordings — audio you record is stored on the operator's server and deleted automatically after a retention period set by the operator (shown in History), unless you mark a lecture as kept or delete it sooner.
  • Transcripts and notes — the text produced from your recordings and any documents or images you add. These are kept until you delete the lecture.
  • Questions you ask in “Ask about the lecture” are answered on the fly and are not stored beyond your current session.

AI providers

Transcription runs on the operator's server. Notes, answers and slide reading may use a cloud AI provider (Claude, Google Gemini or OpenAI) if the operator has enabled one, in which case the text of your lecture is sent to that provider to produce the result; otherwise a local model on the server is used. The operator chooses the provider in Settings.

Google Drive (optional)

You can connect your own Google account so the app saves each lecture's notes, transcript and MP3 into a folder in your Google Drive. When you do:

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission — it can see, edit and delete only the files and folders it created itself, nothing else in your Drive — plus your email address, to show which account is connected.
  • The access token Google issues is stored encrypted on the operator's server and is used solely to upload your lecture files. It is never shared with anyone.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Google Drive, which deletes the stored token and revokes the app's access at Google. Files already in your Drive remain yours; the app never deletes them.
  • You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to any other party.

Email

Your email address is used to sign in and to send account emails (email confirmation, password reset). No marketing email is sent.

Backups

The operator may keep encrypted backups of the server to recover from hardware failure. Deleted lectures disappear from backups as those backups expire.

Your controls

  • Delete any lecture, or only its audio, from History.
  • Change your email or password, or disconnect Google Drive, in Settings.
  • Ask the operator to delete your account and everything associated with it.
