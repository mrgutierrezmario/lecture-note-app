Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

AI Lecture Notes is self-hosted software. The instance you are using is operated by the organisation that issued your account (the “Operator”). This policy describes the information the application processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we store

  • Account information. Your username, email address, and a cryptographically hashed password. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Audio recordings. Recordings are stored on the Operator's server and are deleted automatically after the retention period configured by the Operator (displayed in History), unless you mark a lecture as kept or delete it earlier.
  • Transcripts, notes, and uploaded materials. Text generated from your recordings, together with any documents or images you attach, is retained until you delete the lecture.
  • Questions. Questions submitted through “Ask about the lecture” are answered in real time and are not retained beyond your current session.

2. AI processing

Speech-to-text transcription is performed on the Operator's server. Note generation, question answering, and slide reading may be performed either by a local model on that server or by a third-party AI provider (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or OpenAI) selected by the Operator. When a third-party provider is used, the text of your lecture is transmitted to that provider solely to produce the requested output.

3. Google Drive integration (optional)

You may connect your Google account so that each lecture's notes, transcript, and audio file are saved to a folder in your Google Drive. If you choose to do so:

  • The application requests only the drive.file scope, which permits it to view, create, modify, and delete only the files and folders it has created. It has no access to any other content in your Drive. Your email address is also requested so the connected account can be displayed.
  • The authorization token issued by Google is stored in encrypted form on the Operator's server and is used exclusively to upload your lecture files. It is not shared with any third party.
  • You may disconnect at any time under Settings → Google Drive. Disconnecting deletes the stored token and revokes the application's access with Google. Files already saved to your Drive remain under your control; the application never removes them.
  • Access can also be revoked directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to any other party.

4. Email communications

Your email address is used for authentication and for account-related messages only, such as email confirmation and password reset. No marketing communications are sent.

5. Backups

The Operator may maintain encrypted backups of the server for disaster recovery. Content you delete is removed from backups as those backups expire under the Operator's retention schedule.

6. Your choices

  • Delete any lecture, or its audio recording only, from History.
  • Update your email address or password, or disconnect Google Drive, in Settings.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the Operator.
